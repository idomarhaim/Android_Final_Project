GoalPilot 0.4.1 — the calendar's 3-day view, found on Ido's actual phone

0.4.0 fixed the same class of bug on the Analytics screen. This is the other place
it was hiding, and it was only found because the app was finally driven on a real
Galaxy S25 Ultra instead of on an emulator.

WHAT WAS WRONG

In Calendar, on the "3 days" view, each day gets about a third of the screen. Every
entry drew its time in a fixed 42dp column on the left and a tick circle on the
right, which together take 74dp before the title gets anything at all. On a 384dp
phone that left the title 44dp — so "Write the project book chapter" rendered as

    Write
    the p...
    Stu...

down three lines, with the life area cut off.

WHAT CHANGED

When a lane is too narrow to put the time beside the title, the time now goes
ABOVE it, on one line: "due 20:00", "07:00-09:30", "3 days", "all-day". The title
gets roughly 74dp of a 101dp lane instead of 44, and the tick circle stays exactly
where it was, because ticking work off is the main thing you do on that view.

The wide layout is unchanged wherever there is room for it — Agenda, and 3-day on
a tablet or in landscape.

The real repair is that this choice is now a MEASUREMENT rather than a guess. It
used to be decided by which zoom you were on, which is a stand-in for width that
happens to be right on a wide phone and wrong on a narrow one. Agenda is one column
the full width of the screen; 3-day is a third of it; both were getting the same
layout.

ALSO IN THIS BUILD

Nothing. 0.4.0's four fixes are unchanged and were re-verified on the real phone
before this one was built:

- Goal names in Analytics render on one line (measured: 13dp wide before, 175dp
  after, at the phone's own 384dp and font size 1.15).
- No "#" glyph anywhere; goals without a number say "No number" in words.
- Google Tasks and Health data are in Settings, not on Home.
- Settings opens on Help, with "Replay tutorial" first.

No goals, tasks, calendar entries, life areas or settings are touched by this
update.
