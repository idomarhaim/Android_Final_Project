GoalPilot 0.4.0 — four things Ido reported, fixed

Everything in this build came from one message: things looked wrong on a Galaxy
S25 Ultra that looked fine on the emulator, a "#" nobody could read, two sync
cards in the wrong place, and a tour that pointed at buttons it would not let
you press.

1. GOAL NAMES ARE READABLE AGAIN ON A NARROW PHONE

   On Analytics, under "Window by window", the list of goals with no next step
   put the goal's name, "Schedule the first one" and "Let it go" all on one row.
   The two buttons took their full width first and the name got whatever was
   left — which on a 360dp-wide screen was about one letter, so goal names came
   out stacked vertically, one character per line, eight times over.

   The buttons now sit on their own line underneath. Measured on an emulator set
   to exactly that width: the goal's name went from 13dp wide to 175dp, and it
   cannot be squeezed again at any font size or on any screen, because there is
   no longer anything competing with it for the row.

   Nothing about this was visible on a wider screen, which is why it shipped.

2. THE "#" IS GONE

   A dashed square with a bold "#" in it meant "this goal has no number yet". It
   is a symbol that has to be learned, and there was nowhere to learn it.

   Where the marker sits in a list, in the slot where a percentage would be, it
   now says "No number" in words. Where it is large — on a goal's own screen and
   on the home card — it is simply an empty dashed square, with the sentence
   that was always beside it doing the explaining: "No number yet — nothing
   logged". Screen readers are unaffected; they already read the full sentence.

3. GOOGLE TASKS AND HEALTH DATA MOVED TO SETTINGS

   Both cards are now under Settings > Connected apps, and are gone from Home.
   Nothing about how they work changed, and your Health Connect permission and
   Google Tasks access are untouched.

   Your steps and sleep still sync automatically every time you open the app.
   That never lived on the home screen — only the card reporting it did.

4. THE TOUR NOW LETS YOU PRESS WHAT IT POINTS AT

   Two problems, and the first one was worse than it looked.

   When the tour spotlighted the Calendar tab, tapping it did nothing to the
   Calendar — the tap was absorbed and the tour just moved to the next step. A
   ring around a button that then swallows your press is worse than no ring.

   Now: tap Calendar during the tour and the Calendar actually opens, with your
   real entries in it, and the tour waits there with its explanation on screen
   until you press Next. Press Next instead and it moves on as before — you are
   never made to do it. The only step that still requires a tap is the Goals one,
   as before.

   Second, "Replay tutorial" was already in Settings but was the fourth section
   down, below three tall cards. It is now the first thing in Settings, under
   "Help".

   Because the tour changed, it will run once more even if you have seen it.
   "Skip tour" ends it for good.

Nothing else changed. No goals, tasks, calendar entries, life areas or settings
are touched by this update.
