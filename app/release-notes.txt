GoalPilot 0.5.2 — the app finally looks like it was designed to

Everything in this release was already built. None of it was switched on.

1. THE APP OPENS AS GLASS, NOT AS GREY

GoalPilot has shipped four surface materials for a while — glassmorphism, liquid
glass, soft (neo) and soft dark — each with its own lit background, and 3D volume
on the charts. You could pick them in Settings > Appearance.

The problem was the starting point. A fresh install opened on the flat one, over a
plain unlit ground, with flat charts — so unless you went hunting through Settings,
you never saw any of it. Three defaults that were each defensible on their own added
up to an app that looked like nothing had been designed.

It now opens on glassmorphism, over its own lit background: translucent panels with
a bright rim, the colour showing through from behind. All four materials are still
there in Settings > Appearance, and you can still combine any material with any
background and turn 3D volume on or off.

2. THE CARDS ARRIVE INSTEAD OF APPEARING

Blocks rise and fade in as a screen opens, one after another rather than all at once.

This existed too — on the home screen only. Every other screen in the app had no
motion at all, because the animation is opt-in and only the dashboard had opted in.
Goals, Calendar, Analytics, Challenges, Social, Life areas, Profile and Settings all
have it now.

3. THE HOME-SCREEN WIDGETS ARE PANELS, NOT PLATES

The widgets were opaque grey rectangles sitting on your wallpaper. They are now
translucent, so the wallpaper shows through — which is what the design always
called for.

They stay readable on any wallpaper you have. The panel is exactly as transparent
as it can be while keeping its own text above the accessibility contrast floor
against the worst case in both directions: a pure black wallpaper and a pure white
one. That limit is measured and there is a test holding it.

WHAT HAS NOT CHANGED

Your data, your goals, your points and your settings. If you have already chosen a
material, a background or a relief in Settings, that choice is yours and this
release does not touch it — only the starting point for someone who has never
chosen moves.
