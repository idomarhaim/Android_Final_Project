GoalPilot 0.3.3 — the tour now describes the app you actually have

The guided tour was written before the bottom bar changed. It has been fixed,
and because it changed, it will run once more even if you have already seen it.
Press "Skip tour" and it will not come back.

What is different in the tour:

- Step 6 used to say "Social holds challenges and friends. Profile holds your
  analytics, life areas and achievements", with the whole bottom bar lit up.
  Profile is not in that bar any more — it moved behind your picture, top-right,
  and Calendar took its place. So the tour was naming a tab that was not there.
- Step 6 is now the Calendar tab: what it is for, and the one thing about it you
  cannot see by looking — press and hold a block to pick it up, then drag it to
  another time.
- Step 7 now covers your picture properly: profile, analytics, life areas and
  achievements, plus Settings and Replay tutorial.

Still seven steps, still about half a minute, still skippable at any point.
Settings > Help > Replay tutorial brings it back whenever you like.

Nothing else changed in this build. No goals, tasks, calendar entries or
settings are touched — the only thing this build writes is the note that you
have seen the tour.
