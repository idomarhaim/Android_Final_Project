C22 · What the agent offers a goal that has no number

Prototype for #44 on map #12, revision 1. Three placements for one offer, side by side — they disagree about when a person is receptive, which is the axis, not about what the offer says. Then the sheet the offer opens, and the case that decides the tone: what happens when there is no model. Drawn against the material contract (surface · groove · elevation · accent), so switching material changes the surface and nothing else. Two rules are embodied rather than argued: the absence is stated as legal before anything is offered (C7 — absence is the default), and an offer never borrows the visual language of an outcome — everything here is dashed and hollow, so it can never read as the app having already changed the goal.
