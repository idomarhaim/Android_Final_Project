C19 · What counts as a success and a failure inside a life area

Prototype for #41 on map #12, revision 4. Drawn against the material contract (surface · groove · elevation · accent), so switching material changes the surface and nothing else. Three proposals are embodied here rather than argued: the pair is two numbers, never one rate (C3); a goal nobody ever scheduled is dormant, not failed (C9a — you cannot fail to do something you never agreed to); and nothing ages out of history — the window is a query, not decay (C5 §2). Outcome state never rides on hue: kept is filled, missed is hollow, still-owed is dashed. There is no red on this screen.
