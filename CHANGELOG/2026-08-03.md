### What changed in each

**`AGENTS.md` v8 → v10**
- The board pointer loses its conditional: *"read before your first edit **if
  anyone else may be working here**"* → *"**If this file exists, read it before
  your first edit.** Not 'if someone else may be working here': whether they are
  is what the board tells you."* That exact conditional is what the failing
  session read and reasoned its way past.
- Concurrent-sessions bullet: existence-is-the-trigger, confirm-your-own-row
  (a row another session wrote for you is a report, and it understates your
  footprint), singletons checked before the first **build or device command**,
  and blanket staging banned **outright** rather than "beside a live sibling".
  This repo's project-specific singleton list — Gradle daemon, git index,
  `Pixel_10_Pro_XL`, Firebase `goalpilot-56e30` — was kept and folded into the
  new wording rather than overwritten by the generic template line.
- Subagent-gate bullet now names `Assert-SubagentGate.ps1` and records that
  *"the hook didn't fire" is not approval*.

**`.github/instructions/general.instructions.md` v10 → v12** — this delta is not
from the theming work; it came from the earlier `a1c6ac7` JARVIS session:
- **`AUTO MODE` is sticky** — it starts on a message beginning with `AUTO MODE`
  and stays on for the rest of the session (its absence from a follow-up message
  means nothing), ending only on `NORMAL MODE` or a new session.
- **Pushing is mode-gated**, with six preconditions in auto mode. Applied
  deliberately rather than skipped: those rules are already in force through the
  user's global `my-rules.instructions.md`, which loads in every session
  regardless of what this file says. The stale v10 ("never push without explicit
  OK") was not restraining anything — it was a project doc contradicting the
  global rule, and the contradiction is worse than either version.

**`SESSIONS.md` v1 → v2** — new rule 0 (the file's existence is the trigger),
rule 1 extended to confirming an inherited row, rule 3 an unconditional
blanket-stage ban, rule 4 extended to build/device commands.
