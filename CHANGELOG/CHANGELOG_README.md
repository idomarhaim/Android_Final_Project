- [2026-08-08/c15-language-switching.md](2026-08-08/c15-language-switching.md) — `c15-language-switching` session: resolves [#15](https://github.com/idomarhaim/Android_Final_Project/issues/15) (`C15`), and the headline is that **"language" was three settings wearing one name**. **Language** owns every *word* (chrome, AI text, the §8 fallback, month names) and is stored **per-device beside the skin**, because it must be known before the first frame and the account is not known until Auth resolves. **Region** owns **first day of week** and date order, defaults to the device country, and is **user-overridable and decoupled from language**. **Direction** follows Language, not Region — so English-in-Israel is left-to-right. **Ido overturned this session's sixth question**, which had bundled formats with the week boundary and proposed pinning the week to Sunday: a pinned Sunday is wrong for every other country, and a language-derived week silently **re-buckets analytics history**. Also settled: AI text **follows the picker**, which `C11a`'s 0/10 → 3/3 result prices at one prompt line; the §8 fallback is **authored natively per language, never translated**; the trend chart is **exempt from mirroring**, because `DonutChart` and `ProgressRing` are `Canvas` arcs that cannot mirror at all. Two defects filed as spec lines rather than fixed — no prompt states an output language, and all ten date formatters are process-scoped `val`s that no switch can move. Graduated [#35](https://github.com/idomarhaim/Android_Final_Project/issues/35) (`C15b`) out of the fog.

- [2026-08-10/c4-goal-task-ontology.md](2026-08-10/c4-goal-task-ontology.md) — `c4-goal-task-ontology` session: resolves [#13](https://github.com/idomarhaim/Android_Final_Project/issues/13) (`C4`), the map's root — and **the session's own question picker was overturned before it was answered**. Its four candidate discriminators (measured/done, size, endures/completes, let-the-AI-decide) are all properties of the **object**; Ido's, written the day before in the `E1`–`E19` entity brief, is a property of the **relationship**: a goal is what matters to you *in its own right*, a task and a milestone are both *means*. The proxies do not approximate it — they **invert on his own two examples**, calling unmeasurable *"understand real estate"* a task and perfectly measurable *"finish year 1 of the degree"* a goal, when he says the opposite of both. **Goals do not nest; milestones join them** — "goal" and "milestone" are **roles carried by edges**, an intrinsic one from the user and an instrumental one from another object, so the same object is both at once and promotion either way is adding or dropping **one edge** rather than migrating a document. That converts into a product rule — **the app may act silently on instrumental structure, but must ask before asserting an intrinsic edge** — which answers `R4` by removing its premise: the smart sorter must never invent a goal, and today it does so unconditionally, even on the `confidence: 0` fallback path whose confidence the client never reads. **Two things found by checking rather than assuming:** that fallback path, and that the "free" unfiled-task inbox is not free — an unfiled task is **counted on the dashboard and listed on no screen**, so it costs one surface. Filed [#37](https://github.com/idomarhaim/Android_Final_Project/issues/37) `C16` the milestone entity, [#38](https://github.com/idomarhaim/Android_Final_Project/issues/38) `C17` many-to-many linkage, [#39](https://github.com/idomarhaim/Android_Final_Project/issues/39) `C18` sub-task depth; folded `E9`'s third-goal-kind invitation into `C5` and `E4`'s success/failure view into the fog. **Closing the root left the map more blocked, not less** — of the four tickets `#13` was blocking, exactly one became takeable, and two acquired new blockers from this very resolution.
