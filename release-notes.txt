GoalPilot — #57 b: backgrounds are a setting now

Settings > Appearance gains a Background control: Match / Glow / Spectrum /
Plain, combinable with all four materials. Each tile previews the current
material sitting on that ground, and the material tiles above preview on the
ground you have selected.

Two older defects are fixed in the same build, and both change how the app
looks:

1. Every screen was painting an opaque panel over the app's background, so the
   per-material grounds have never actually been visible. They are now, on
   every screen.
2. Glass in dark mode failed the accessibility contrast standard on its own
   background (body text at 2.6:1, where 4.5:1 is required). Glass and liquid
   glass in dark mode now have slightly deeper panels on a slightly quieter
   ground. Light mode, Soft and Soft dark are untouched.

Note: choosing a lit ground under Soft or Soft dark deliberately stops it being
neumorphism — the panel becomes a translucent plate and gains an edge. The app
tells you so under the picker before you pick. The same happens in reverse:
glass on a Plain ground has nothing to be transparent about.

717 unit tests and 194 instrumented tests green.
