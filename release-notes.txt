GoalPilot 0.3.2 — the app now shows you how it works

The first time you open this build, a short guided tour starts by itself. It
dims the screen, puts a spotlight on one thing at a time, and explains it in a
sentence — your points card, the quick-add box, the Goals tab, the new-goal
button, the rest of the bottom bar, and where your account and settings live.

Seven steps, about half a minute.

- You can skip it at any point. Tap "Skip tour", or just press Back.
- One step waits for you: it asks you to tap the Goals tab yourself, and then
  follows you there.
- Tapping anywhere on the dimmed area moves to the next step.
- It only opens once. To see it again — Settings > Help > Replay tutorial.
  Replaying changes nothing in your data.

If you turn animations off in Android's accessibility settings, the tour is
still there, without the pulsing highlight.

Nothing else changed in this build: no goals, tasks or settings are touched.

782 unit tests and 223 instrumented tests green.
