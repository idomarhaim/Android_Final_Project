v0.3.0 — Settings, and a whole new Appearance

This is the release that gives GoalPilot a real Settings screen. Open it from the
avatar at the top of the home screen.

APPEARANCE
- Four materials to draw the app out of: glass, liquid glass, neo and dark neo.
  Every screen re-renders in the one you pick.
- Dark neo is dark-only, and the app says so on the tile rather than just
  greying the brightness control. Your brightness choice is remembered and comes
  back the moment you pick another material.
- Colour themes and light/dark as before, now in one place.

LANGUAGE & REGION
- Choose the language GoalPilot speaks. Your own goals, tasks and life-area
  names stay exactly as you wrote them.
- Region sets which day your week starts on and how a date reads. The screen
  shows you the answer for the region you picked, not a description of it.

YOUR DAY
- Set the hours you are awake, and when GoalPilot plans tomorrow. The planning
  time follows your waking hours until you override it.

AI — bring your own API key (optional)
- GoalPilot answers with its own free model, and always will. You can now add
  your own GROQ, OpenAI, Anthropic or Google Gemini key instead.
- Same features, same screens, just a better answerer. Nothing needs a key.
- The key is encrypted on this phone. It is never saved to your account and
  never included in a backup, so it does not follow you to a new device.
- A status line always says which model actually answered. If your key is
  rejected or out of quota, the free model answers and the screen tells you so
  rather than going quiet.
- Remove the key any time and everything carries on.

EVERYTHING HERE BELONGS TO THIS PHONE
Nothing on the Settings screen is tied to your account, and none of it changes
when you sign out.
